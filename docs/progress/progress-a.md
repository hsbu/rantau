## Agreed foundation decisions and integration boundaries

- The requester approved the S-01/S-02/A-01 proposal on 2026-10-16 and confirmed the product name Rantau. This file consolidates the former foundation notes and implementation proposal; executable field definitions remain in `backend/contracts/`, with product requirements in the MVP/roadmap.
- Money uses strict nonnegative JavaScript-safe integers: IDR scale 0 (whole rupiah), CNY scale 2 (fen). Payment amounts are positive. Quote rates are exact decimal strings in destination major units per source major unit, rounded half up to destination minor units; totals equal principal plus fee. Times are timezone-aware. `terms_hash` is an opaque trusted-service digest, never generated authority from the agent.
